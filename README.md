About Me
I am a Technology and Operations Director, business partner, Systems Engineer and AI Architect based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
I currently lead operations, technology and digital transformation for an education company, connecting business strategy with software architecture, process automation, data, cloud infrastructure and operational intelligence.
My professional experience spans Brazil, Texas and Miami, including work connected to White Stone Concrete in Texas, HID in Miami, and technology and operations initiatives in Brazil.
I started primarily with Python and expanded my engineering work into C#, .NET, C++, Java, TypeScript, Go and Rust. My current focus is the design of reliable systems that integrate software, artificial intelligence, data, security and business operations.
I do not see technology as an isolated department. I see it as an operational capability that must generate measurable value.
